# Conventional HF/DFT: independent no-RI evidence

Large conventional **no-auxiliary-RI** RHF and RKS/PBE now have real results.
PBE AO reuse reduces the matched warm median by **27.806%**, with a substantial
memory tradeoff. RHF requires separating the failed original PySCF workflow
from an explicitly different fresh-density control. Production remains
**`1a575d18`, unchanged by this diagnostic round**.

The case is C40H56, 96 atoms, 880 Cartesian def2-SVP AO; KS uses grid3.
Runs are CPU float64, 64 logical CPUs, in existing allocation 115927/n006.
The atom guesses are independently generated by each engine. Numerical gates,
200-main-cycle budget and direct screening threshold `1e-12` are unchanged.
[conventional_summary.json](conventional_summary.json) retains raw hashes;
[conventional_results.csv](conventional_results.csv) separates workflow,
diagnostic, kernel and compiler-estimate scopes.

All calls use 22 cycles and record the same energy, -1554.702526705899 Ha,
and gradient `8.915603876315022e-8 Ha`. Warm time falls by **27.806%**; peak
RSS rises by **32.218 GB**. Facade timing includes preparation and SCF. RSS
covers each complete four-call worker, not an isolated warm call. This is a
clear time/memory tradeoff, not a memory-reduction result.
The independent conventional PBE PySCF reference returned after **1477.186588 s**

9.313226e-10 Ha, density RMS 3.497530e-10 and orbital gradient 8.301916e-8 Ha.
The energy is -1554.7025268841403 Ha, about 1.782e-7 Ha from GradSCF. It stopped
after the first failed logical call, so this is one attempted call out of four,
not four failed repeats or a timeout. No conventional cross-engine PBE speed
ratio is accepted, and the RI reference's 211 s timing is not substituted.

They return `converged=false, scientific_pass=false`: **none is a convergence
baseline**. Their diagnostic facade times are RHF 93.321624 s, SVWN 167.906056 s,
PBE0 302.147276 s and CAM-B3LYP 370.955073 s. Inclusive nested spans are not additive.

## AO retention and budget interpretation

| Materializer scope | Preparation (s) | Compiler output (decimal GB) | Compiler temporary (decimal GB) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| SVWN/LDA | 13.062593 | 7.878 | 0.197 |
| PBE0/GGA | 101.253001 | 31.405 | 0.537 |
| CAM/GGA | 101.062461 | 31.405 | 0.537 |

The output/temporary figures are compile-only buffer estimates, not actual
RSS and not additional executed AO allocations. The current optional cache
reserves two retained-size buffers plus scratch. For this geometry, two complete
GGA buffers require roughly **62.7 GB**, exceeding the nominal **48 GB**
workspace of a 60,000-decimal-MB default before resident arrays. The source
therefore keeps streaming at that budget. The actual benchmark uses
180,000 MB and measures about 37.3 GB RSS; **this is not a measured 60 GB run**.
The two-copy admission rule is conservative even where a compiler estimate
suggests smaller scratch. [Cache admission:187-215](../../../src/gradscf/scf/inputs/grid.py#L187-L215).
Forward compiler estimates do not bound reverse-AD tapes, nuclear Hessians or
opaque native allocations. A budget-planner change still needs its own gates.

## Source findings versus pending timing

The ordinary quartet mask is **basis-only Schwarz screening**, not density
screening. Density enters accumulation after quartet generation. With fixed
geometry/cutoff, using a density increment does not reduce generated quartets
through this mask. [Traversal:103-119](../../../src/gradscf/integrals/_native/csrc/coulomb_shells.h#L103-L119).
Optimizer and Schwarz-bound owners are call-scoped and recreated for each native
call; their runtime share has not been measured separately.
[Owners:39-43](../../../src/gradscf/integrals/_native/csrc/direct_jk.cc#L39-L43).

`max_memory` does reach the bound provider's private-output quota; these are
not invariably fixed-64-MiB calls. Active-worker selection is explicitly capped
by requested workers, pool size, shell rows and funded output quota. This
source fact does not establish runtime parallel efficiency.
[Quota:27-38](../../../src/gradscf/integrals/backends/native/workspace.py#L27-L38),
[worker selection:27-35](../../../src/gradscf/integrals/_native/csrc/direct_jk.cc#L27-L35).

The same-geometry amplitude probe is complete, with one fixed provider,
1 GiB private quota, cutoff 1e-12 and 64 requested workers. Median combined
J/K times are **10.607566 s at D** and **10.650405 s at 1e-4 D**, three warm
calls each. The small/full time ratio is 1.00404. Linearity passes (maximum
J/K differences 9.498e-17 / 4.662e-18). Four orders of density reduction did
not reduce runtime in this test, consistent with the density-independent
quartet mask. No runtime quartet counter, selected-worker count or complete
SCF speedup is inferred. These scaled matrices are operator inputs, not two
normalized physical densities. Production source is unchanged.

The direct optimization priorities therefore include fixed-basis preparation
reuse and a separately validated SCF density-screening policy, rather than
assuming that incremental matrix accumulation already saves ERI generation.
Any numerical preparation cache must invalidate with geometry, basis values
and operator changes. Density-dependent screening must preserve the existing
array, basis, geometry and neural-training derivative contracts; it cannot be
copied blindly into linear-response products.
