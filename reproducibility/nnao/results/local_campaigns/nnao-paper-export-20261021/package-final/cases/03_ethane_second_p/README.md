# Ethane with a second carbon valence-p radial channel

## Methods

Method: All-electron DF-RHF, def2-universal-jkfit. Cohort: `free-contraction-only/update-3351-geometry`. Exact settings, commands, hardware and original status fields remain in the imported source records. Numerical source files are copied without editing.

## Results

25 checked geometries with 50 rather than 44 AOs. Fifteen angles were independently optimized and ten were transferred by exact reflection/atom permutation and rechecked. Free grid barrier: 3.133362078 kcal/mol.

Archive status: `complete`. Exported geometry/state records: **50**.

## Interpretation boundary

The 44-AO MACE checkpoint was not enlarged or retrained. Transferred points are not independent optimizations; no interpolation was used.

## Files

- `structures/`: generated standard XYZ with Angstrom coordinates, charge and 2S in the comment. Missing state information is explicitly `None`, not assigned a guessed state.
- `artifacts/`: source data, coefficients, plots, scripts and protocol records with their original relative paths. Original-language notes are retained as source records.
- `../../structure-index.json`: coordinate/state provenance and aliases; `../../FILE_MANIFEST.json`: original paths and SHA256 checksums.
