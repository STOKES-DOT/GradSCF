Case 08 molecular structures

166 existing xyzrender images in 21 groups: six S66x8 dimers, six HB300SPXx10 dimers, and nine BH9 reactions. Charge and 2S labels follow the source manifest. This gallery contains structures, not new calculations.

Dimer renders share a camera and physical pixel scale within each group. Distance scale factors are dimensionless. Reactant and product fragments are separate source XYZ structures. Species are independently oriented, with a common scale within each reaction. Inferred bonds are visual guides only. No combined fragment geometry is constructed.

One common foreground crop is used per group; original PNG/SVG files are unchanged. Representatives are framed individually; displayed sizes are not comparable across groups.
